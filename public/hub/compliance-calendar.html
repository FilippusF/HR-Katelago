Compliance calendar

Every deadline a Namibian employer carries

Monthly, annual and rolling obligations in one place, with the section of the law behind each one. We keep this page current, and we say plainly when something changes.

Last reviewed 25 August 2026 by the Katelago HR team

Changing now

The Social Security contribution ceiling rises from N$11,000 to N$12,500 a month, which takes the maximum monthly contribution to N$112.50 each for employer and employee. Government Notice 236 of 2026 states an effective date of 1 March 2026, but the Social Security Commission has confirmed it will apply from 1 September 2026 and will not be backdated. Update your payroll for the September run.

VAT registered businesses also file the VAT return by the 25th of the month following the end of each two month tax period. It is not an employment obligation, but it sits on the same payroll calendar for most of our clients.

Annual obligations

Annual obligations
WhenObligationAuthority
1 January National minimum wage step up. The wage order sets rising minimums to 1 January 2027. Check your lowest paid categories every January, and remember that payments in kind such as food, clothing or housing may not be set off against the minimum wage. Wage Order GN 218 of 2024, as amended
1 to 31 March Employees Compensation wage statement. A certified statement of wages paid from 1 March of the previous year to the last day of February. Understating wages attracts a penalty of up to ten percent of the difference. New employers becoming liable after 31 March submit within one month. Employees Compensation Act 30 of 1941, section 68
By about 30 March PAYE annual reconciliation and employee tax certificates. A return of all employees, total remuneration and total employees tax, plus a PAYE 5 certificate to every employee, within 30 days after the tax year ending on the last day of February. Income Tax Act 24 of 1981, Schedule 2
Within 30 days of assessment Employees Compensation assessment. The Commission assesses employers after 31 March. Payment falls due within 30 days of the date on the assessment notice. Employees Compensation Act 30 of 1941, sections 69 and 72
Within 31 days of 31 March Employer Training Grant claim. Not an obligation, but money left on the table if you miss it. Levy paying employers can claim back up to half of the levies paid. Late applications forfeit the grant. Namibia Training Authority
30 June Individual income tax returns for salaried employees. Business individuals and farmers file by 30 September. Worth flagging to your workforce. Income Tax Act 24 of 1981

Rolling deadlines: Affirmative Action reporting

Affirmative Action reporting does not have a national deadline. Your due date is twelve months from your own last submission, which is exactly why it gets missed.

Affirmative Action reporting
ItemRequirement
Who must reportRelevant employers, meaning employers with 10 or more employees, in all sectors, since 15 September 2022.
First reportForm EEC 1, within 18 months of becoming a relevant employer.
Further reportsForm EEC 2, every 12 months after the previous submission.
The planA three year affirmative action plan prepared in consultation with an elected employee committee, with minutes and the statistical analysis kept on file.
ExtensionsApply with reasons at least two months before the due date.
Compliance certificateValid until the Commission issues the next one, a review panel finally disapproves your next report, or you fail to submit a further report once 12 months have passed. Without a valid certificate you cannot receive a State contract, guarantee, loan, licence, permit, grant or concession.

Authority: Affirmative Action (Employment) Act 29 of 1998, sections 23 to 28, 41 and 42. Designated groups are racially disadvantaged persons, women irrespective of race, and persons with disabilities.

Section 34 of the Labour Act has no headcount threshold. A single redundancy for economic, technological or re-organisation reasons triggers the full process, and failing to comply is a criminal offence.
