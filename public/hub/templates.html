How to use these properly

A template gets the structure right. It cannot know your facts, your policy, your collective agreement or the history between the people involved. Those are the things that decide cases.

Use them as a starting point, read them before you send them, and change what does not fit. If the matter is serious, have someone check it.

Every template on this page is general guidance and not tailored legal advice. Katelago accepts no liability for the use of a template in circumstances it was not drafted for. Where a dismissal, a retrenchment or a statutory deadline is involved, get advice on your specific situation.
