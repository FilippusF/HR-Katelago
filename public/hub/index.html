Payroll alert

The Social Security contribution ceiling moves from N$11,000 to N$12,500 a month, taking the maximum contribution to N$112.50 each for employer and employee. The Social Security Commission has confirmed implementation from 1 September 2026, even though the gazette states 1 March 2026. Update your payroll now. See the compliance calendar

What is due, and when

Open the full calendar

The obligations every Namibian employer carries, in one place, with the section of the law behind each one. This is the page to bookmark.

Monthly, by the 20th

PAYE return and payment

Employees tax deducted and remitted to NamRA on form PAYE 4, within 20 days after month end.

Monthly, by the 20th

VET Levy

One percent of payroll for employers with an annual payroll of N$1 million or more, cleared in the National Training Fund account.

Monthly, within 30 days

Social Security contributions

0.9 percent employer and 0.9 percent employee of basic wage, with the return on Form 10.

Annual, 1 to 31 March

Employees Compensation return

Certified wage statement for the year to the end of February, under section 68 of the Employees Compensation Act.
